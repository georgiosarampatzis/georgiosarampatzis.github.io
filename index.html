About

I am an NLP researcher and Informatics educator working on multilingual natural language processing, information retrieval and trustworthy text generation.

My research focuses on humour-aware language technologies, Business Intelligence, business-oriented text analytics, cross-lingual retrieval, pun translation, biomedical text simplification, sentiment analysis and evaluation of large language models. I have participated in international shared tasks including CLEF, SemEval and TREC.
